Customer ID, Product ID and Loan ID are permanent system identifiers. Mobile numbers are contact fields only and are never used as the permanent identity.

6. Consolidated Due Sheet — Multi-Loan Safe

Every loan is shown as its own row/record. Customer details and guarantor details come from that loan's historical snapshot, so two loans belonging to the same customer can safely have different guarantors, primary mobiles, secondary mobiles and addresses.
